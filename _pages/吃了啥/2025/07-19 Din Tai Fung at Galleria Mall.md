Today I visited Din Tai Fung, the world-renowned Taiwanese restaurant chain that recently opened its branch at Galleria Mall. This restaurant has built its reputation on exquisite dim sum, particularly their meticulously crafted xiaolongbao (soup dumplings).

The moment you enter, you can tell this isn't your average Chinese restaurant. The sleek, modern interior and open kitchen concept where you can watch the chefs expertly fold dumplings with precisely 18 pleats (their signature standard) sets the tone for a premium dining experience.

![](/assets/img/2025/06-07/Din Tai Fung.jpg)

The menu offers a comprehensive selection of authentic Chinese dishes. We ordered their famous xiaolongbao, which arrived in a traditional bamboo steamer, alongside spicy wontons, fried rice, sautéed vegetables, and dan dan noodles. Each dish was impeccably prepared and presented, showcasing why Din Tai Fung has earned Michelin stars at some of its locations worldwide.

The xiaolongbao deserves special mention - these delicate soup-filled dumplings were perfectly thin-skinned with a rich, flavorful broth inside. The technique for eating them properly (placing them on your spoon, nibbling the skin to release the soup, then enjoying the rest) is part of the unique dining experience.

While Din Tai Fung is undeniably on the expensive side for Chinese cuisine, the quality justifies the price. The precision in preparation, freshness of ingredients, and consistency across dishes demonstrate why this restaurant has gained such international acclaim. The service was attentive and efficient, enhancing the overall experience.

For those looking to experience authentic, high-quality Chinese cuisine in an elegant setting, Din Tai Fung at Galleria Mall delivers a memorable dining experience well worth the splurge.
