Today I visited Madang Korean Restaurant, an upscale dining establishment uniquely situated within a golf playground. This restaurant offers an authentic Korean culinary experience in a refined setting.

The meal began with an impressive array of banchan (side dishes) that showcased traditional Korean flavors - kimchi, pickled vegetables, and various small bites that prepare the palate for the main courses. The attention to detail in these small dishes was a promising indication of what was to come.

![](/assets/img/2025/06-07/Madang Korean Restaurant 01.jpeg)

We ordered a variety of classic Korean dishes, including a bubbling hot pot filled with rice cakes, vegetables, and eggs, as well as perfectly fried Korean chicken with various coatings. The restaurant also offers premium cuts of meat for tabletop grilling, beautifully presented and accompanied by fresh vegetables and dipping sauces.

![](/assets/img/2025/06-07/Madang Korean Restaurant 02.jpeg)

One of the highlights was the bingsoo (Korean shaved ice dessert) topped with sweet red beans and condensed milk - a refreshing conclusion to a flavorful meal. The restaurant also takes pride in their presentation, with gold utensils adding a touch of luxury to the dining experience.

![](/assets/img/2025/06-07/Madang Korean Restaurant 03.jpeg)

The restaurant's location within a golf playground creates a unique atmosphere, combining the tranquility of the surroundings with the lively experience of Korean dining. The interior features elegant dark tables and traditional Korean design elements, creating an ambiance that balances authenticity with sophistication.

While Madang is certainly positioned in the higher price bracket for Korean cuisine, the quality of ingredients, generous portions, and exceptional preparation justify the expense. It's an excellent choice for those seeking an authentic yet elevated Korean dining experience or for special occasions when you want to impress guests with something beyond the ordinary.

For lovers of Korean cuisine who appreciate quality and are willing to pay a premium for it, Madang offers a dining experience that satisfies both culinary expectations and the desire for an elegant setting.
